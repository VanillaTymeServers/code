## Shop Branding

%R1.8%, your shop or its branding must not imply association or be easily confused with any other person or organization. \
We ask that you change your shop's [Name](%PROJECT_SETTINGS_LINK%) and other relevant branding to avoid causing confusion or implying association with existing shops or individuals. \
When editing your shop's Name, remember to update its [URL](%PROJECT_SETTINGS_LINK%) to match.
