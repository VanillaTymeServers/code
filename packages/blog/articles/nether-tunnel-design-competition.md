It's that time again! Season 11 is on its way, and we need a fresh nether tunnel design for the new map, and once again **you** get to design it.

Submit your build, then the community votes on their favorite. The winning design will be built into all four of our main nether tunnels, and its designer gets their own Collectible.

## What should it look like?

That's up to you. We have a few rules below, but beyond those we want your best and most creative ideas.

Don't worry too much about how much the materials cost. Our community is strong and resources are plentiful. Just keep it realistic: as fun as it would be, we can't build tunnels out of diamond or netherite blocks.

## The template

Every design starts from the same template, and all designs are built in **single player**. [Download the template schematic](./Season11TunnelTemplate.litematic), load it into a single player world with [Litematica](https://modrinth.com/mod/litematica), and build your design on top of it. More details are on the signs around the template.

## Building rules

Your design has to fit the template and keep the tunnel safe and easy to travel through.

### Size and layout

- **Blue walls** are your design space. They can be up to **8 blocks tall**, unless you want the bedrock ceiling as part of your design. The template has a bedrock border to guide you.
- The **yellow layer** under the floor is also yours to design, but it must be exactly **1 block thick**.
- The **red sections** are doorways to personal tunnels, and each one is **5 blocks wide**. You can cover them with a solid block, or use fences and gates to keep mobs out.

### Safety

- The tunnel must be **spawn proof**. The floor has to include **glass, slabs or carpet**, plus an **ice path**.
- The ice path must be **regular ice only**. Packed ice and blue ice let mobs spawn.
- Place **trapdoors above the ice path**, on the top side of the second block. Check the template for reference.

### Travel

- The tunnel must be **elytra friendly**. At least part of it needs a smooth open area where players can fly without catching on stairs, walls, chains, lanterns or similar blocks.
